Dataset generations & corrections

This is the study's second-generation dataset at two full replicates per configuration: every active record came through the shipped, reproducible runner (Claude Code headless path with answer validation and retry), and each config's 12 tasks were run twice (sample tags cc1/cc2; a few haiku@medium cells carry a third early sample). The first-generation records (a session-bound orchestration harness) are preserved in archive/workflow-runs/. Replication materially revised three of our own published claims — Opus 4.8@low's perfect sweep, Sonnet@xhigh's ledger crossing, and "max effort never wins" — all documented in F4/F5. Two grading corrections carry forward: JSON-wrapped answers are graded on content, and transport failures are quarantined as invalid rather than scored.

Limits

n=2 is still small — ranges are honest but confidence intervals are not computable; the plan is to accumulate replicates over time (one runner/sweep.py --sample auto per pass). All runs came from one account over three days on subscription serving; costs are imputed from measured tokens at standard list prices. Tasks are synthetic, single-shot, ≤35 minutes, without repository context; long-horizon autonomy and agentic orchestration are not measured. Response-length headroom was raised to the models' own output ceilings; harnesses with tighter caps will fail the biggest grinds. Usage-window exhaustion during long sweeps produces transport failures — the sweep driver cleans and refills them automatically.
