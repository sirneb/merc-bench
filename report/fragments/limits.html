Dataset generations & corrections

This is the study's second-generation dataset: every active record was produced through the shipped, reproducible runner (Claude Code headless path with answer validation and retry), re-measuring the full grid that the first-generation study ran through a session-bound orchestration harness. The first-generation records are preserved in archive/workflow-runs/; headline agreements (the Opus 5@medium floor, the Sonnet xhigh ledger crossing, Haiku's deep-puzzle collapse at high effort, Opus 4.8's subtle-bugs stall) and disagreements (Fable@low's zero-defect sweep did not replicate; Opus 4.8@low swept perfect here) are discussed in the findings. Two grading corrections from the first generation carry forward: JSON-wrapped answers are graded on content (the original T4 "effort ladder" was an artifact of scoring the wrapper), and transport failures are quarantined as invalid rather than scored.

Limits

Every active cell is n=1 (haiku@medium n=2 on several tasks) from a single account over two days on subscription serving; costs are imputed from measured tokens at standard list prices. Tasks are synthetic, single-shot, ≤35 minutes, without repository context; long-horizon autonomy and agentic orchestration are not measured. Response-length headroom was raised to the models' own output ceilings; harnesses with tighter caps will fail the biggest grinds. Rerun before trusting fine margins — one command per config reruns any cell.
