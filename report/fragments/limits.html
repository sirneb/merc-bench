Dataset generations & corrections

This is the study's second-generation dataset at two full replicates per configuration: every active record came through the shipped, reproducible runner (Claude Code headless path with answer validation and retry), and each config's 12 tasks were run twice (sample tags cc1/cc2; a few haiku@medium cells carry a third early sample). Fable 5.1 (2026-09-01→02) and Opus 5.5 (2026-09-26) were added after the fact through the same runner at n=2; one Fable 5.1 max-effort replicate hit a burst of transport failures that were quarantined as invalid and refilled on a later pass. The first-generation records (a session-bound orchestration harness) are preserved in archive/workflow-runs/. Replication materially revised three of our own published claims — Opus 4.8@low's perfect sweep, Sonnet@xhigh's ledger crossing, and "max effort never wins" — all documented in F4/F5. Two grading corrections carry forward: JSON-wrapped answers are graded on content, and transport failures are quarantined as invalid rather than scored. Two cost corrections landed 2026-09-27 and re-priced every record from stored token usage: Sonnet 5 was billed at $3/$15 instead of its $2/$10 list price (all Sonnet costs fell ~25%), and cache writes under the Claude Code harness were billed at the 5-minute rate (1.25× input) when claude -p writes 1-hour caches (2× input), which raised every other family's sweep cost 6–43% (most at low effort, where cache writes dominate). Neither correction moved any floor. The runner now also records which model actually served each attempt and quarantines records answered by a fallback model after a safety-classifier refusal — a failure mode discovered during the hard-tier tournament (see candidates/TOURNAMENT.md on the hard-tier-candidates branch).

Limits

n=2 is still small — ranges are honest but confidence intervals are not computable; the plan is to accumulate replicates over time (one runner/sweep.py --sample auto per pass). All runs came from one account on subscription serving, in three measurement windows: the original six families within three days of each other in July 2026, Fable 5.1 five weeks later, Opus 5.5 four weeks after that — so serving-side drift between windows is not controlled, and cross-window comparisons (Opus 5 vs Opus 5.5, Fable 5 vs Fable 5.1) carry that caveat. Costs are imputed from measured tokens at standard list prices; every family's price row and the 1-hour cache-write rate were verified against the CLI's list-basis cost fields, and recomputed costs match the CLI's own total_cost_usd to the cent. Tasks are synthetic, single-shot, ≤35 minutes, without repository context; long-horizon autonomy and agentic orchestration are not measured. Response-length headroom was raised to the models' own output ceilings; harnesses with tighter caps will fail the biggest grinds. Usage-window exhaustion during long sweeps produces transport failures — the sweep driver cleans and refills them automatically.
