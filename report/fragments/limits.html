Corrections

This dataset supersedes two earlier grading decisions, both found by re-auditing and both correctable by anyone from the shipped raw answers: (1) constraint-gauntlet (T4) scores were initially under-graded for runs that returned JSON-wrapped text — the wrapper was scored as content, producing an apparent "effort ladder" (3/10→10/10 on Sonnet) that disappears when the unwrapped essays are graded; T4 is in fact near-saturated for every tier above Haiku. (2) A small number of answer-format deviations (prose instead of schema JSON) are parsed tolerantly and graded on content, with format non-compliance noted rather than punished. The graders in tasks/ implement the corrected policy; make verify reproduces every published number from raw answers.

Limits

Most cells are n=1; frontier configurations carry n≤3 plus repeated puzzle attempts — treat single-cell gaps as directional and cross-task patterns as the findings. Tasks are synthetic, single-shot, ≤60 minutes, without repository context; long-horizon autonomy and agentic orchestration are not measured here, which is exactly where the priciest configurations claim their value. A minority of records lack usage data (a logging fault in two replicate batches); their costs are null and excluded from totals, and totals containing estimated cells are marked ≈. Costs use standard list prices (Sonnet's introductory pricing would flatten ~⅓ off its rows through 2026-08-31). All original runs came from one account over three days via Claude Code subagents; serving stacks change — rerun before trusting fine margins.
