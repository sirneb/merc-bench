H1 · Three tasks, one real separator

TALLY-12 and Quarry Duel are solved cleanly in both replicates by thirteen configurations each; cold-chain by seven. Cold-chain — a 30-day, four-depot perishable-inventory simulation with ratio-based rationing — is where the frontier splits: Opus 5.5 holds it from high up, Fable 5.1 only from xhigh, Sonnet 5.5 at medium, xhigh and max, and nobody else twice at any effort. Opus 5 and Opus 4.8 land at 100 % in one replicate and 20–50 % in the other at almost every effort; Fable 5 never holds it twice. The failure is always one bookkeeping slip around a stockout day that propagates to the end.

H2 · Opus 5.5 is alone at the top, and cheap there

Opus 5.5 is the only configuration family clean on all three tasks in both replicates, and it does so at high, xhigh and max. At high the three-task sweep costs $2.37 and 13 minutes; Fable 5.1, the only other model to reach the same place, needs xhigh and $11.33 / 31 minutes. Opus 5.5 at medium is already clean on TALLY-12 and 99 % on cold-chain; its only gap below high is Quarry Duel (79 %, one collapse in three runs).

H3 · The effort axis finally shows, in both directions

Haiku 4.5 never leaves the noise floor (3–17 % at every effort, and its low-effort Quarry Duel attempts often fail to produce JSON at all). Below medium every Sonnet collapses to the same noise (1–5 %); at low the Opus and Fable tiers still solve TALLY-12 and Quarry Duel but not cold-chain. Above medium the two Sonnets diverge: Sonnet 5.5 improves through xhigh (cold-chain clean, TALLY-12 85–90 %) and then falls off a cliff at max — 0 % on TALLY-12 in both replicates, because thinking consumes the 128k output budget and no answer arrives. Sonnet 5 peaks at medium (75–81 % everywhere) and degrades from high onwards, ending with two budget overruns at max. Max effort never improved a Sonnet on this tier; it bought Opus 5.5 and Fable 5.1 nothing they did not already have at xhigh.

H4 · Replicates matter more here than on the core set

The hard-tier graders award partial credit, but a single state slip cascades through everything after it, so the same configuration can score 30 % and 100 % on consecutive runs — Opus 5 at medium on Quarry Duel, Fable 5 at high on cold-chain, Sonnet 5.5 at low on all three. Treat any n=2 cell with a spread wider than 30 points as "unreliable here", not as a mean. P(clean) in the table is the honest summary.

H5 · What the hard tier does not change

The ten core tasks still decide the reliability floors, and those floors did not move: Opus 5 @ medium, Opus 5.5 @ xhigh, Fable 5.1 @ xhigh, Sonnet 5 @ max — joined by the new Sonnet 5.5 @ xhigh, which at $1.29 is now the cheapest replicated-clean config in the grid. The hard tier re-orders the top: on tasks that actually separate the frontier, Opus 5.5 is a clear first, Fable 5.1 second at four to five times the price, Sonnet 5.5 third with one task it cannot hold, and Opus 5 — the core-set value champion — fourth, undone by cold-chain.
